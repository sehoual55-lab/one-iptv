One Smart TV App: Complete Guide to Smart One IPTV Setup, Features and Activation

Updated 2026-11-16

The one smart tv app most people search for is Smart One IPTV, a media player for smart TVs that plays playlists you already have from a licensed provider. It does not include any channels on its own. This guide explains what the app does, how to download and activate it, what to check before you pay, and how it compares with other players. Note that several unrelated apps called "One TV" also appear in app stores, so this guide covers how to tell them apart.

What Is the One Smart TV App?

Smart One IPTV is a playlist player for smart TVs and streaming devices. You load a playlist from your own legal TV provider, and the app organizes it into channels, movies and series.

The app is easy to confuse with similarly named apps, so it helps to know which one you are looking at:

  • Smart One IPTV (often called "Smart One" or "IPTV Smart One"): An IPTV player for Samsung, LG, Android TV, Fire TV and other TV platforms. It is the main subject of this article.
  • "One TV" apps on Google Play or APKPure: These are separate apps from other developers. Some are entertainment guides or video discovery apps, and they are not IPTV players.
  • TV channels named "One": If you want to watch a channel such as BBC One, you usually need the broadcaster's own app rather than an IPTV player. See the FAQ below.

IPTV means television delivered over an internet connection instead of an antenna, cable or satellite. For background, see Wikipedia's overview of Internet Protocol television.

Key Features of the Smart One IPTV App

Smart One focuses on organizing and playing your playlist on a TV screen. Its main features cover multi-device management, playback control and personalization.

Core features

  • Wide TV compatibility: The app runs on Samsung (Tizen), LG (webOS), Android TV, Amazon Fire OS, and some Vidaa and Vestel models.
  • Multiple playlists and accounts: You can store more than one playlist and switch between them.
  • Fast zapping: You can move quickly between live channels.
  • Advanced video player: It includes options for aspect ratio, audio tracks and subtitles, where the stream provides them.
  • Favorites and hidden categories: You can pin the channels you use most and hide groups you never watch.
  • TV archive (catch-up): You can replay past programs if your provider supports catch-up.
  • Parental lock: You can PIN-protect selected categories.
  • Movie and series details: It shows posters and descriptions pulled from TMDb metadata.
  • Extras: These include internet radio stations, live sports scores, multiple interface languages and color themes.
  • Web-based management: You manage your TVs and playlists from the official website instead of typing long URLs with a remote.

What to look for in any IPTV player

Before you commit to Smart One or any alternative, check these points:

  1. Your TV is supported. Confirm your TV brand and operating system version are on the supported list.
  2. Your playlist format is accepted. Most providers give an M3U link or login details, so check that the player accepts the format you have.
  3. There is a free trial. A trial lets you test playback on your own network before paying.
  4. The interface is easy to use with a remote. Large menus and quick search matter more on a TV than on a phone.
  5. Updates are regular. Smart TV operating systems change over time, and maintained apps keep working.

How to Choose the Right Option

Choose Smart One if you watch on a Samsung or LG TV and want one player that also works on your other TVs. Choose a different player if you only use Android devices and want deeper customization.

Ask yourself three questions:

  • Which devices do you use? Smart One's strength is native support on Tizen and webOS, where fewer players are available. On Android TV or Fire TV you have many more choices.
  • Do you have a legal content source? The app is only a player. You need a subscription or playlist from a licensed provider, or free legal streams.
  • Do you prefer a one-time fee or a free app? Smart One uses a paid activation after its trial. Some alternatives are free with fewer features.

If you also stream on a games console, our guide to setting up and watching IPTV on Xbox explains which options work there.

Step-by-Step: How to Get Started With Smart One

You install the app from your TV's app store, note the device ID shown on screen, add your playlist through the official website, and then restart the app.

1. Download the Smart One app

  • Samsung: Open Apps from the home screen, search for "Smart One IPTV," and install it. If you need help finding or installing apps, Samsung Support has guides for each TV model.
  • LG: Open the LG Content Store (called "Apps" on newer webOS versions), search for "Smart One," and install it.
  • Android TV / Google TV: Install it from the Google Play Store. The listing is called "SmartOne IPTV Player."
  • Fire TV: Search the Amazon Appstore. If the app is not listed in your region, use only the official download method that the developer documents.

Avoid unofficial APK sites. Copies on those sites may be outdated or modified.

2. Note your device identifier

When you first open the app, it shows a device ID or MAC-style address on screen. Write this down exactly as shown, because it links your TV to your account.

3. Add your playlist

  1. Go to the official Smart One website.
  2. Enter your device identifier.
  3. Paste the playlist link or login details from your legal provider.
  4. Give the playlist a name.

4. Restart and load content

Close and reopen the app on your TV. Your channels, movies and series should load. Large playlists can take a minute on the first launch.

5. Complete the iptv smart one activation

When the trial ends, you activate the app through the official website using your device identifier. After payment, the TV linked to that identifier is unlocked.

Pricing and Plans

Smart One offers a free trial, then requires a paid activation per device. Check the official website for the current price, because it can change and may vary by region.

Keep these points in mind:

  • Activation is per device. If you have two TVs, you will usually need to activate each one.
  • The app fee and your content subscription are separate. Paying for Smart One does not give you any channels.
  • Buy only from official channels. Pay through the developer's website or the app store, not through third-party "activation codes" sold elsewhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I watch One on my smart TV?

It depends on what "One" means to you.

  • If you mean the Smart One IPTV app: Install it from your TV's app store, note the device ID, add your legal playlist on the official website, and restart the app.
  • If you mean a TV channel such as BBC One: Use the broadcaster's official app. In the UK, that is BBC iPlayer, which is available on most smart TVs and requires a TV Licence for live viewing.
  • If you mean a "One TV" app: Search for the exact app name in your TV's app store and check the developer's name before installing.

Is the smart one app free?

You can try it for free for a limited period. After that, you need a paid activation for each device.

Does Smart One IPTV include channels?

No. It is a player only. You need a playlist from a licensed provider or from legal free sources.

Where can I get a safe smart one app download?

Download it from the Samsung or LG app store, Google Play, or the Amazon Appstore. Avoid unofficial APK mirrors.

Can I use one activation on several TVs?

Usually not. Activation is tied to a single device identifier, so each TV needs its own.

Is using Smart One IPTV legal?

Yes. The player itself is legal software. Whether your viewing is legal depends on the content you load, so only use playlists from providers who hold the rights to what they offer.

Conclusion

The one smart tv app known as Smart One IPTV is a capable, TV-friendly player, especially for Samsung and LG owners who have few other good options. To get started, install it from an official store, use the trial with a legal playlist, and activate it only if it suits your setup.
